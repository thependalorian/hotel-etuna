# Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** May 17, 2026  
**Policy Owner:** Executive Sponsor  
**Review Frequency:** Annual  
**TSC Reference:** CC2.1  
**Approval Required:** Executive Sponsor (CEO/Owner)

---

## 1. Purpose

Set ethical standards for Hotel Etuna and Buffr personnel when serving guests, partners, regulators, and each other.

## 2. Scope

All employees, contractors, directors, and representatives acting on behalf of Hotel Etuna or the Buffr Host platform.


## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Hotel Etuna** | Hub hospitality platform and operating entity |
| **Personnel** | Employees, contractors, and partners with access to Hotel Etuna systems |
| **Production** | Live environment serving guests and partners (Vercel + Neon production branch) |

## 3. Core principles

### 3.1 Integrity and honesty

- Provide accurate information to guests, auditors, and regulators.
- Do not falsify bookings, payments, tax records, or compliance evidence.
- Disclose errors promptly and cooperate with remediation.

### 3.2 Respect and non-discrimination

- Treat guests and colleagues with dignity regardless of nationality, gender, religion, disability, or status.
- Zero tolerance for harassment, bullying, or retaliation.

### 3.3 Conflicts of interest

- Disclose financial or personal relationships that could influence vendor selection, staffing, or guest treatment.
- Do not accept gifts or favours from vendors or guests beyond modest hospitality without management approval.
- Do not use company systems for personal commercial gain.

### 3.4 Confidentiality

- Protect guest PII and business secrets per Data Classification and Data Protection policies.
- Do not discuss guest stays or payment details on social media.

### 3.5 Lawful conduct

- Comply with Namibian law including labour, tax, FICA/AML obligations, PSD-12, and ETA record-keeping.
- Report suspected money laundering or fraud to the designated compliance officer; do not tip off subjects.

### 3.6 Use of company resources

- Follow Acceptable Use Policy for devices, email, and internet.
- Platform admin privileges are for legitimate business only.

## 4. Reporting violations

- Report concerns to line manager, HR, or CTO in confidence.
- Serious matters (fraud, harassment, data breach cover-up) may be reported directly to Executive Sponsor.
- No retaliation against good-faith reporters.

## 5. Consequences

Violations may result in coaching, disciplinary action, termination, civil liability, or referral to law enforcement/regulators as appropriate.
