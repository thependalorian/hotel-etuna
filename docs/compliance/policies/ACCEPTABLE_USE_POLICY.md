# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** May 16, 2026  
**Policy Owner:** CTO  
**Review Frequency:** Annual  
**TSC Reference:** CC2.1  
**Approval Required:** Executive Sponsor (CEO/Owner)

---

## 1. Purpose

Define acceptable use of Hotel Etuna information systems, email, and guest data by all personnel.

## 2. Scope

All staff, contractors, and partners with system access.

## 3. Acceptable use

Users **may**:

- Access systems required for their role
- Use corporate email for legitimate business
- Store work files in approved tools (GitHub, Vercel, 1Password, Neon console via SSO)

## 4. Prohibited use

Users **must not**:

- Share credentials or bypass MFA
- Install unapproved software on devices accessing production
- Exfiltrate guest PII to personal email, USB, or cloud drives
- Use guest data for personal purposes or marketing without consent
- Probe, scan, or attack systems without written authorization
- Mine cryptocurrency on company infrastructure
- Harass colleagues or guests via systems
- Disable logging, RLS, or security controls in production
- Commit secrets to git or share API keys in chat

## 5. Guest-facing systems

- Sofia AI must not be prompted to bypass policy (prices for unauthenticated users, etc.) — follow product rules.
- Social media posts about guests require consent.

## 6. Monitoring

Hotel Etuna may monitor system use per [`INFORMATION_SECURITY_POLICY.md`](INFORMATION_SECURITY_POLICY.md) and law. No expectation of privacy on company systems.

## 7. Violations

Report violations to CTO. Violations may result in access revocation, discipline, or law enforcement referral.
