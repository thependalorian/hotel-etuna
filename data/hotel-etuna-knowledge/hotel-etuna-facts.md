# Hotel Etuna Facts

Hotel Etuna is a luxury guesthouse in Ongwediva, Namibia. The name "Etuna" means "He takes care of us" in Oshiwambo. We offer 5 room types: Standard, Luxury, Family, Executive Suite, and Premier. Address: 5544 Valley Street, Ongwediva. Phone: +264 65 231 177. Email: info@hoteletuna.com. Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 11:00. Free WiFi, outdoor pool, free parking, on-site restaurant, 24-hour security, airport shuttle (paid). NFC payments are accepted on the property. All rooms have air conditioning, mosquito nets, satellite TV, minibar, and private bathroom. The restaurant serves breakfast from 07:00 to 10:00. Lunch, dinner, and bar orders are available from 10:00 to 22:00. The menu includes light meals, main courses (lamb, beef, pork, poultry, seafood), traditional Namibian cuisine (oshingali, omaungu, ombidi, okapana), pizzas, sharing platters, desserts, and a full bar with wine list. Signature items include full breakfast, oxtail, king klip, lamb curry, and Etuna-branded pizzas. Located 500 metres from the Ongwediva Trade Fair Centre.
