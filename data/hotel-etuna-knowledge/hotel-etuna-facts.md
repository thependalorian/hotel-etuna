# Hotel Etuna Facts

Hotel Etuna is a luxury guesthouse in Ongwediva, Namibia. The name "Etuna" means "He takes care of us" in Oshiwambo. We have **35 guest rooms** in five categories: Standard Room Type A (N$800, double bed), Type B (N$800, two singles), Type C (N$1,200, double + single, 3 guests), Executive Room (N$1,000), and Premiere Room (N$2,000). **Conference Hall** bookings are N$1,200 per session (08:00–17:00). **Campsite** whole-site hire: N$250 per Namibian guest, N$400 per non-Namibian guest, minimum N$1,200 total. Address: 5544 Valley Street, Ongwediva. Phone: +264 65 231 177. Email: frontdesk@hoteletuna.com (reservations); marketing@hoteletuna.com; support@hoteletuna.com; admin@hoteletuna.com; founder@hoteletuna.com. Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 11:00. Free WiFi, outdoor pool, free parking, on-site restaurant, 24-hour security, airport shuttle (paid). We accept cash, card (swipe or tap), and secure online card payments; dining charges can also be added to your room bill. All rooms have air conditioning, mosquito nets, satellite TV, minibar, and private bathroom. The restaurant serves breakfast from 07:00 to 10:00. Lunch, dinner, and bar orders are available from 10:00 to 22:00. The menu includes light meals, main courses (lamb, beef, pork, poultry, seafood), traditional Namibian cuisine (oshingali, omaungu, ombidi, okapana), pizzas, sharing platters, desserts, and a full bar with wine list. Signature items include full breakfast, oxtail, king klip, lamb curry, and Etuna-branded pizzas. Located 500 metres from the Ongwediva Trade Fair Centre.
