# Room Descriptions

Hotel Etuna offers **35 guest rooms** across **five marketing categories**: Standard Room (Types A, B, C), Executive Room, and Premiere Room.

## Standard Room (Type A) — N$800/night — Double bed
Rooms **5, 6, 8, 17, 19, 21**. Essential comfort with one double bed, air conditioning, mosquito net, satellite TV, coffee/tea station, mini fridge, and private bathroom with shower.

## Standard Room (Type B) — N$800/night — Two single beds
Rooms **7, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20**. Same Standard category with two single beds — ideal for friends or colleagues travelling together.

## Standard Room (Type C) — N$1,200/night — Double and single bed
Rooms **2, 3, 4**. Double bed plus single bed for up to **three guests** (e.g. parent with two children).

## Executive Room — N$1,000/night
Rooms **22, E1–E6, E8–E13**. Business-friendly room with work desk, premium toiletries, VIP lounge access, air conditioning, and private bathroom.

## Premiere Room — N$2,000/night
Rooms **E7, E14**. Flagship stay with private lounge, master bedroom, second room with twin beds, balcony, and space for up to four guests.

## Conference Hall / Facilities — N$1,200 per session
**08:00–17:00** per calendar day. Book online at `/facilities/conference`.

## Campsite — whole-site hire
**N$250** per Namibian guest, **N$400** per non-Namibian guest; **whole-site total = max(N$1,200, sum of per-person charges)**. Book online at `/facilities/campsite`.

All guest rooms include free WiFi, mini fridge, mosquito nets, and daily housekeeping.
