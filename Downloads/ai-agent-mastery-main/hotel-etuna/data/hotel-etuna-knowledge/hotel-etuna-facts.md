# Hotel Etuna Facts

Hotel Etuna is a luxury guesthouse in Ongwediva, Namibia. The name "Etuna" means "He takes care of us" in Oshiwambo. We offer 5 room types: Standard, Luxury, Family, Executive Suite, and Premier. Address: 5544 Valley of the Leopard Street, Ongwediva. Phone: +264 65 231 177. Email: info@hoteletuna.com. Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 11:00. Free WiFi, outdoor pool, free parking, on-site restaurant, 24-hour security, airport shuttle (paid). NFC payments are accepted on the property. All rooms have air conditioning, mosquito nets, satellite TV, minibar, and private bathroom. The restaurant serves breakfast buffet from 06:30 to 10:00, dinner from 18:00 to 22:00. Traditional dishes include oshifima with spinach, Zambezi bream, and potjie. We offer cultural tours, nature excursions, and airport shuttles. Located 500 metres from the Ongwediva Trade Fair Centre.
