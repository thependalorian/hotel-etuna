# Local Area Guide

Hotel Etuna is 500 metres from Ongwediva Trade Fair Centre. Nearest airport: Oshakati Airport (30 km). Pick n Pay Ongwediva is 600 metres away. The main road B1 is 2 km away. The property is in a quiet area but close to the town centre. Taxis are available nearby. The area is safe for walking. Common Oshiwambo greetings: Moro (Hello), Wa lalapo? (How are you?), Nangalei po (Goodbye). The currency is Namibian Dollar (NAD), and South African Rand is also accepted. The weather is generally warm and dry, with summer rains from November to March.
