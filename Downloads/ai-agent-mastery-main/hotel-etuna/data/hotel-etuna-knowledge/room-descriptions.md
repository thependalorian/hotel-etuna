# Room Descriptions

## Standard Room
Essential comfort, air conditioning, mosquito net, satellite TV, coffee/tea station, private bathroom with shower.

## Luxury Room
More space, elegant touches, premium bathroom, sitting area, pool view.

## Family Room
Two double beds, garden access, extra bedding, ideal for families.

## Executive Suite
26 square metres, work desk, premium toiletries, VIP lounge access, perfect for business travellers.

## Premier Room
Two bedrooms, two bathrooms, private lounge, balcony, sleeps up to 6 guests.

All rooms include free WiFi, minibar, bathrobes, and daily housekeeping.
